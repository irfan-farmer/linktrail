LinkTrail Settings

What LinkTrail stores: one record per link you've had open, containing its address, its title, and when you first and last saw it. Everything stays on this device in the browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded, and there are no servers, accounts, analytics, or network requests. Private/incognito windows are never recorded. Changing a privacy setting below also cleans up data that was already saved.
